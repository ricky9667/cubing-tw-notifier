# Cubing TW notifier

The bot announces Cubing TW competition updates to subscribed Discord servers and a Telegram channel.

## Language

**Discord subscription**:
A server's choice of one channel for competition alerts and, optionally, one role to tag in those alerts.

**Mention role**:
The optional Discord role selected with `/subscribe` for tagging in every competition alert sent to that server.

**Competition alert**:
A notice about a new competition, registration opening, or a competition starting the next day.
